# QuickStack+ — detailed trailer script

Target: 44 seconds, 16:9, 60 FPS. Tone: relatable inventory mess followed by one satisfying action.

## Capture preparation

- Build an organized storage room with chests, trapped chests, and barrels within 16 blocks.
- Seed containers with clear categories: stone, ores, crops, wood, and mob drops.
- Fill the player inventory with matching stacks plus three unmatched valuables.
- Craft the Sorting Amulet normally for the hero take.
- Setup-only fallback: `/function x1_quickstack:core/give_amulet`.
- Record a clean before and after inventory screenshot for editorial verification.

## Required proof shots

- The recipe uses an iron chain, two redstone, one amethyst shard, and one chest.
- The destination container already contains the matching item before activation.
- Unmatched items remain.
- The amulet is returned after activation.
- Chests, trapped chests, and barrels are all shown without claiming support for other storage blocks.

## Reset between takes

Restore the exact “before” inventory and container counts. Retakes are otherwise impossible to compare cleanly. Keep a text note of every starting stack.

## Deliverables

- Full English trailer.
- 12-second vertical cut: messy inventory → amulet craft → activation → clean inventory.
- Thumbnail candidate: amulet between a cluttered inventory and a clean storage wall.
